Phase 1 foundations. `@waldow-ds/tokens` adds three-tier DTCG tokens with light and dark themes and a WCAG AA contrast gate. `@waldow-ds/ui` adds Button, Input, Field, Select, Checkbox, Card, Stack, Badge, Dialog and Table on shadcn/ui and Radix, styled only with tokens. `@waldow-ds/catalog` is reserved for the Phase 2 component catalog.
